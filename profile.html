WinWave – Terms & Conditions

WinWave istemal karne se pehle in Terms & Conditions ko ghour se parhein. App ko use karne ka matlab hai ke aap in tamam sharaait se mutafiq hain.

1. Withdrawal Policy
  • Withdrawal request dene ke liye user ke account mein kam az kam 5 valid members hona zaroori hai.
  • Agar 5 valid members mukammal na hon to withdrawal approve nahi ki jayegi.
2. Deposit Requirement
  • Jis din user withdrawal request submit karega, usi date par user ka minimum required deposit hona lazmi hai.
  • Misal ke taur par agar withdrawal aaj lagayi ja rahi hai to aaj ki date mein bhi ek valid deposit record hona chahiye.
  • Agar withdrawal kisi aur din lagayi jaye to us din ki date par bhi ek valid deposit hona zaroori hai.
  • Deposit ke baghair withdrawal request process nahi ki jayegi.
3. Verification
  • WinWave kisi bhi withdrawal ya account ko verify karne ka haq rakhta hai.
  • Ghalat maloomat, fake accounts ya fraudulent activity ki surat mein account suspend ya terminate kiya ja sakta hai.
4. Rules Ki Pabandi
  • Tamam users WinWave ke rules aur policies ki pabandi karne ke paband hain.
  • Rules ki khilaf warzi ki surat mein withdrawal reject ki ja sakti hai ya account band kiya ja sakta hai.
5. Terms Mein Tabdeeli
  • WinWave baghair pehle notice ke in Terms & Conditions mein tabdeeli karne ka haq rakhta hai.
  • Naye rules website ya app par publish hote hi nafiz samjhe jayenge.

WinWave ko use karne se aap in tamam Terms & Conditions ko tasleem karte hain.
